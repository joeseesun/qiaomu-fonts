# 手稿来源 · Manuscript references

发布的是重新绘制的现代拉丁展示字体。参考图像由下列机构提供在线浏览，仓库保存字形源文件、字体和样张。各套字体的笔画比例、弧度和字距经过排版调整。手稿里缺少的字符由自绘字形或 Punchcutter 的参数化字形补齐。

The collection contains new Latin display fonts informed by historical manuscript images. The links below identify the pages used as visual references. The downloadable files contain font outlines, generated specimens, and editable font sources.

| Font | Manuscript inspected | Design choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trivulzio Quill | Leonardo da Vinci, [Codex Trivulzianus, fol. 30r](https://www.cite-sciences.fr/au-programme/expos-temporaires/leonard-de-vinci/manuscrits-leonard-vinci/acc/lexicon-profile-man.html), Archivio Storico Civico e Biblioteca Trivulziana, Milan. | The supplied mirror-script page was flipped for inspection. Both cases and the digits were redrawn around tapering entries and long loops. The preview can flip whole lines. |
| Chawton Script | Jane Austen, [*Lady Susan*, manuscript p. 28](https://janeausten.ac.uk/manuscripts/lady_susan/28.html), Jane Austen’s Fiction Manuscripts Digital Edition. Original at [The Morgan Library & Museum](https://www.themorgan.org/exhibitions/online/jane-austen/3). | Low x-height, pronounced rightward slant and rising hairlines. All lowercase plus selected capitals were drawn for the family. |
| Arles Letter | Vincent van Gogh, [letter to Theo, Arles, 16 October 1888, recto](https://www.vangoghmuseum.nl/nl/brieven/collectie/b0599V1962), Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam (Vincent van Gogh Foundation). | Round, pressure-heavy strokes, short exits, open forms and a more upright appearance. All lowercase plus selected capitals were drawn for the family. |
| Monticello Draft | Thomas Jefferson, [rough draft of the Declaration of Independence, June 1776, p. 1](https://www.loc.gov/resource/mtj1.001_0545_0548/?sp=1), Library of Congress, Manuscript Division, Thomas Jefferson Papers. | Narrow lowercase with compact loops and steep curves. The reference was the main text hand. All lowercase plus selected capitals were drawn for the family. |
| Amherst Pencil | Emily Dickinson, late 1850s pencil manuscripts: [A 651, *Her breast is fit for pearls*](https://www.emilydickinson.org/basic/mutilations/susans-name-removed/her-breast-is-fit-for-pearls) (Amherst College); [H B189, *Success is counted sweetest*](https://archive.emilydickinson.org/working/hb189.htm) and [H 259, *Going to Heaven!*](https://archive.emilydickinson.org/working/h259.htm), [front](https://archive.emilydickinson.org/working/zh259a.htm) and [back](https://archive.emilydickinson.org/working/zh259b.htm) (Houghton Library, Harvard University). | Four written sides were compared. Slender graphite strokes, slightly uneven baselines, and the rising dash informed the new lettering. All lowercase and uppercase, plus the three dash lengths, were redrawn. |
| Trinity Notes | Isaac Newton, [Trinity College Notebook, Cambridge University Library MS Add.3996](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/trinity-college-notebook-ms-add-3996-newton-isaac-sir-1642-1727/FQEZOxO2IZfebA). The Latin text on digital pages [106](https://images.lib.cam.ac.uk/iiif/MS-ADD-03996-000-00104.jp2/full/1050,/0/default.jpg), [107](https://images.lib.cam.ac.uk/iiif/MS-ADD-03996-000-00105.jp2/full/1050,/0/default.jpg), [108](https://images.lib.cam.ac.uk/iiif/MS-ADD-03996-000-00106.jp2/full/1050,/0/default.jpg) and [109](https://images.lib.cam.ac.uk/iiif/MS-ADD-03996-000-00107.jp2/full/1050,/0/default.jpg), plus the scientific notes on [183](https://images.lib.cam.ac.uk/iiif/MS-ADD-03996-000-00181.jp2/full/1050,/0/default.jpg) and [184](https://images.lib.cam.ac.uk/iiif/MS-ADD-03996-000-00182.jp2/full/1050,/0/default.jpg). | The Latin pages informed the small, compact lowercase and pointed turns. The scientific pages supplied numeral references. All lowercase and uppercase, digits and the historical long s (`ſ`) were redrawn. |

Punchcutter 1.3.1 supplies the pen expansion, spacing, kerning, character coverage, font compilation and specimens. Its [upstream repository](https://github.com/byrishi/punchcutter) is MIT-licensed. Bundled Studio pages include the [upstream MIT notice](punchcutter-MIT.txt). The [OFL](../OFL.txt) covers this repository’s font software and font sources.
